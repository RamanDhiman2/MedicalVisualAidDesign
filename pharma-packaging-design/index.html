Regulatory Standards

100% CDSCO Packaging Compliance Guarantee

Every design strictly incorporates mandatory statutory elements required by drug inspectors and testing authorities across India:

01. Salt generic name prominently sized relative to brand name
02. Prominent Red warning box for Schedule G, H, H1 & X drugs
03. Clear Rx, NRx, and XRx symbols placed in the top-left quadrant
04. Standard Mfg Lic No, Batch No, Mfg Date, and Expiry slots
05. Manufacturer & Marketer full address with corporate pin code
06. High-contrast GS1 Barcodes & dynamic 2D DataMatrix codes

Pharma Packaging Design FAQs

Do I get 3D mockups of my box before mass printing?

Yes. Every packaging package includes high-resolution 3D digital box renders from multiple angles. You can use these 3D renders immediately for social media, digital catalogs, and distributor presentations.

Can you work with our local printing press specifications?

Yes. Simply share your printer's die-line (CAD drawing) or cutter dimensions, and we configure bleed, safety margins, color separations, and trapping specifically for their offset machinery.

What is your turnaround time for a medicine box design?

Initial design concepts with 3D mockups are presented within 24 to 48 hours. Print-ready vector CDR and PDF files are released promptly after revisions.
