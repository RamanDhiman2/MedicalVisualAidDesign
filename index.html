FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is included in your pharma visual aid design service?

We design complete doctor detailing visual aids—including hardbound folders, spiral-bound books, tablet-friendly digital e-visual aids, and leave-behind leaflets (LBL). All layouts include salt compositions, therapeutic indications, mechanism of action (MOA) illustrations, and brand highlights.

Do you provide print-ready source files (CDR, PDF, AI)?

Yes. You receive high-resolution, print-ready PDFs and editable vector source files (CorelDraw CDR, Adobe Illustrator AI) configured with proper bleed, crop marks, and CMYK color profiles that any printing press can produce directly.

Can you design PCD pharma marketing kits for new startups?

Absolutely. We specialize in end-to-end promotional kits for new PCD pharma companies: brand logo, visual aid, reminder cards, catch covers, product glossary, order books, visiting cards, and MR bags.

Do you design regulatory-compliant medicine packaging & labels?

Yes. Every medicine mono carton, blister pack foil, and syrup bottle label is designed adhering to CDSCO packaging norms—with accurate placement for Rx symbol, salt composition, dosage, warnings, batch details, and barcode/QR codes.

How do 3D product renders help before printing?

Photorealistic 3D product renders allow you to launch marketing campaigns, list medicines on your website, create product brochures, and pitch to distributors even before your first physical manufacturing run is complete.

Do you serve clients in Chandigarh, Baddi, Ahmedabad, and Pan-India?

Yes. While we work closely with major pharma manufacturing hubs like Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, Baddi, and Ahmedabad, we deliver design services to pharma franchise companies pan-India and globally via digital handover.

Can you run Meta & Google Ads for PCD franchise leads?

Yes. We design high-converting ad creatives, build targeted landing pages, and run lead-generation campaigns on Google and Facebook/Instagram to deliver genuine PCD pharma franchise and distributor inquiries.

How long does a visual aid design project take?

A standard 12-20 page visual aid typically takes 3 to 5 business days for first concepts, with prompt revisions based on your brand requirements.
