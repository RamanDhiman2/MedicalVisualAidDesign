Frequently Asked Questions

Pharma Visual Aid Design FAQs

What files do I receive once the visual aid is complete?

You receive 100% editable CorelDraw (CDR) or Adobe Illustrator (AI) source files, along with CMYK 300 DPI high-resolution press-ready PDFs configured with bleeds and crop marks ready for any printing press in India.

Do you help write or summarize salt compositions and medical indications?

Yes. You can provide your product list with salts, dosages, and brand names. We research standard pharmacological mechanisms of action (MOA), clinical indications, and key doctor selling propositions to craft persuasive headlines.

Can you redesign an existing visual aid that looks outdated?

Absolutely. We frequently modernize legacy pharma visual aids with contemporary scientific typography, 3D medicine renders, clean infographic flowcharts, and vibrant color coding for different therapeutic segments.
